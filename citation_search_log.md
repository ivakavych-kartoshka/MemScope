## Inclusion criteria

1. Direct relevance to scoped/persistent agent memory, its attacks, mobile execution, long-horizon evaluation, or the formal authorization/lifecycle basis.
2. Verifiable existence and metadata from an official scholarly or platform source.
3. The manuscript uses no claim stronger than the inspected abstract, official metadata, API reference, theorem framing, or full text.
4. Platform pages support only statements about documented platform behavior.
5. Conference/journal versions are preferred when verified; arXiv is retained for recent work without a verified archival version.

## Exclusion criteria

- Unresolved author metadata, title/identifier mismatch, inaccessible or ambiguous artifact, duplicate version, neighboring topic without a supporting sentence, marketing-only performance claim, or source that does not support the intended claim.
